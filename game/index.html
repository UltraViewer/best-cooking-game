How to play

  1. Read the order board — each ticket shows a dish and the ingredients it needs. The bar is the customer's patience.
  2. Pick up ingredients from the pantry (click, or keys 1–7). You carry one thing at a time.
  3. Chop chili, chicken and greens on the board (click it, or C). Cook rice, egg and noodles on the stove (click a burner, or B) — take food off before it burns!
  4. Plate it (click the plate, or P). When the plate matches a recipe, it lights up as a finished dish.
  5. Serve at the window (click it, or S) before the customer's patience runs out. Faster serves earn bigger tips.
  6. Shop between days: sharper knives, more burners, patient customers, golden spatula.
  7. Miss 3 orders and the run ends. Days get faster and hungrier.
